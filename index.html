Julio Sebastián Zárate Vásquez, Ph.D.

Researcher in Science & Technology Studies — data governance, open science, digital commons, and Andean agrobiodiversity.

About

Julio Sebastián Zárate Vásquez is a interdisciplinary social scientist working on the social, environmental, and political dimensions of science and technology (STS) in Latin America.

His research critically examines open knowledge infrastructures, data justice, and the sovereignty of indigenous communities in the face of expanding digital platforms. Working through ethnography and quantitave data analysis, he collaborates with local and international networks to rethink the cooperative conservation of Andean agricultural biodiversity under principles of responsible innovation and knowledge commons.

Projects & Networks

He is an active member of the SEEKCommons Network, and has contributed to the project "Exploring the Challenges of Opening Agrobiodiversity Knowledge Infrastructures in the Andes," which examined technological infrastructures, socio-environmental justice, and data sovereignty in Andean commons.

He is also part of the collective initiative behind Asociación Sidereus Nuncius and the emerging STS Peru Network, working to institutionalize and connect Social Studies of Science, Technology, and Society in Peru through interdisciplinary spaces that bring together art, computational design, and science policy.

His ethnographic collaborations with the International Potato Center (CIP) and the GMO Observatory focus on the conservation dynamics of native potato germplasm in high-altitude communities, alongside the regulatory and institutional monitoring of gene editing.
